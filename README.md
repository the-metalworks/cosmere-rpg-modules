# Cosmere RPG Foundry VTT Modules Issue and Features
A place to report and track bugs, feature requests, etc. for the released modules of the Cosmere RPG Foundry VTT implementation.
## **Supported Products**
The follow sections and tables provide a list of Cosmere RPG products which currently have a module implementation.
### Core Rulebooks
|Name|Description|Marketplace|
|---|---|---|
|Handbook|The more player-facing core rules for the Cosmere RPG and the corresponding setting. Contains the majority of the rules needed to play.|[Stormlight](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-stormlight-handbook), [Mistborn](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-mistborn-handbook)|
World Guide|The more GM-oriented core rules for the Cosmere RPG and the corresponding setting. They include tons of additional lore, art, and a huge bestiary of adversaries to face!|[Stormlight](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-stormlight-worldguide), [Mistborn](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-mistborn-worldguide)|
### Adventures
|Setting|Name|Description|Starting Level|Ending Level|Link(s)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Stormlight|The First Step|A 'level 0' adventure where players begin with blank character sheets. Players gain paths, talents, skills, and expertises through choices made. |Stormlight|Speak the Words|When Odium's forces besiege the city of Karanak, a group of heroes face a life-or-death decision. |Stormlight|Law and Obligation|On what they thought was a routine task to deliver a message, a group of heroes must protect a budding Radiant from a skilled and powerful foe. |Stormlight|Stonewalkers|A full-campaign adventure that shows off many locations across Roshar through a canonical adventure which ties _Words of Radiance_ and _Wind in Truth_ together through the players' actions.|1|8|[Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-stormlight-stonewalkers)|
|Mistborn|Legacy|A full-campaign adventure that spans both era 1 **and** era 2 of the Mistborn setting.
