# Cosmere RPG Foundry VTT Modules Issue and Features
A place to report and track bugs, feature requests, etc. for the released modules of the Cosmere RPG Foundry VTT implementation.
## **Supported Products**
The follow sections and tables provide a list of Cosmere RPG products which currently have a module implementation.
### Core Rulebooks
Name | Description | Link(s)
-- | -- | --
Stormlight Handbook | The more player-facing core rules for the Cosmere RPG and the Stormlight setting and contains the majority of the rules needed to play. | [Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-stormlight-handbook)
Stormlight World Guide | The more GM-oriented core rules for the Cosmere RPG and the Stormlight setting. It includes tons of additional lore, art, and a huge bestiary of adversaries to face! | [Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-stormlight-worldguide)
Mistborn Handbook | The more player-facing core rules for the Cosmere RPG and the Mistborn setting and contains the majority of the rules needed to play. | [Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-mistborn-handbook)
Mistborn World Guide | The more GM-oriented core rules for the Cosmere RPG and the Mistborn setting. It includes tons of additional lore, art, and a huge bestiary of adversaries to face! | [Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-mistborn-worldguide)
### Adventures
Setting | Name | Description | Levels | Link(s)

Stormlight | Bridge Nine | An introductory adventure for both the Cosmere RPG and the Stormlight setting, providing both excellent set-pieces and guidance for both new GMs and players. | 1-2 | [Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-stormlight-bridge-nine)
Stormlight | Stonewalkers | A full-campaign adventure that shows off many locations across Roshar through a canonical adventure which ties _Words of Radiance_ and _Wind in Truth_ together through the players' actions. | 1–8 | [Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-stormlight-stonewalkers)
Stormlight | The First Step | A 'level 0' adventure where players begin with blank character sheets. Players gain paths, talents, skills, and expertises through choices made. Stormlight | Speak the Words | When Odium's forces besiege the city of Karanak, a group of heroes face a life-or-death decision. Whether they choose protection or liberation, at least one hero will have a chance to swear the First Ideal and become Radiant. | 3 | [Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-stormlight-speak-the-words)
Stormlight | Law and Obligation | On what they thought was a routine task to deliver a message, a group of heroes must protect a budding Radiant from a skilled and powerful foe. Can they get to him first, and can they convince him to embrace a difficult truth? | 3 | [Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-stormlight-law-and-obligation)
Mistborn | Legacy | A full-campaign adventure that spans both era 1 **and** era 2 of the Mistborn setting. Players control a party in each era, with the choices of era 1's party affecting the events of era 2. | 1–6, 3–9 | [Foundry Marketplace](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/cosmere-rpg-mistborn-legacy)
